Meet the women behind HILINDA BROILERS

HILINDA BROILERS started with two sisters,Linda Zoka and Hilda Zoka , who were tired of guessing whether the chicken they bought was actually clean, actually healthy, or actually worth the price. So they decided to run it themselves — the way they'd want it done.

Every bird is checked for health before it ever reaches a customer. Every cleaned chicken goes through the same strict hygiene routine, every single time — no shortcuts, no exceptions. And every price is set to be fair, both to the families buying from us and the small farms we buy from.

"We treat every order like it's going to our own family's table — because to us, this business is personal."

— Founded and run day-to-day by our two co-founders, right here in Ribe, Kilifi County.
